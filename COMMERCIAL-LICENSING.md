# Commercial licensing and services

Tungsten is available under AGPL-3.0 and under separately agreed commercial terms.

The AGPL option permits use, modification, and distribution, including commercial
use, provided its requirements are met. See [LICENSE](LICENSE) for the full text.

Projects that need to use Tungsten in proprietary or closed-source software
without complying with AGPL requirements can contact **contact@myoon.sk** for a
commercial license. Early licenses are handled case by case; there is no fixed
public pricing. A commercial license is granted only by a separate agreement.
This document describes availability and does not itself grant commercial rights.

Paid services are available directly from the developer:

- Custom development
- Integration into existing applications
- Custom components and features
- Priority support
- Consulting

Contact [contact@myoon.sk](mailto:contact@myoon.sk) with your project, integration
requirements, and expected deployment to discuss scope and terms.

## Contributions and dual licensing

Before accepting external code contributions, establish a CLA or another rights
agreement sufficient to distribute those contributions under both AGPL-3.0 and
the commercial license. Do not assume an AGPL-only contribution grants the right
to commercially relicense it. The full agreement and acceptance workflow remain
release preparation work; see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
